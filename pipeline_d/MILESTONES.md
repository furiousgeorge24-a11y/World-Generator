## M0 — self-contained bootstrap

Purpose: freeze success before any candidate output can redefine it.

Required artifacts:

- self-contained README, contract, design obligations, evaluation protocol,
  milestone plan, value ledger, and attempt register;
- a bootstrap manifest recording every authorized inherited or shared item
  and every deliberate exclusion;
- a C-specific adapter boundary for the declared shared root WebUI shell;
- fail-closed adapter behavior: the two-control interface is visible for
  review, but `ready: false` blocks generation and placeholder output;
- C-specific evaluation schemas, prompts, instruments, and packaging;
- quarantine checks preventing undeclared cross-pipeline imports or future
  consultation;
- documentation and harness validation.

Exit criteria:

1. `target_land_percent` semantics are fixed at 0–70 with a per-map
   ±10-percentage-point tolerance, including the explicit endpoint intervals.
2. `landmass_fragmentation` semantics are fixed at 0–1 without an island-count
   promise; zero means a strong likelihood of one dominant macro-landmass
   when enough land exists, while small natural secondary islands remain
   valid.
3. Same-seed common randomness, fixed-window behavior, causal border,
   natural-process restrictions, and prohibited shortcuts are explicit and
   testable.
4. Deterministic conformance gates are separated from calibrated behavioral
   instruments and perceptual morphology review.
5. The bootstrap contains no land generator, output gallery, or success
   claim.

M0's documentation is not evidence that the requested model is feasible.

## M1 — land-target architecture

Purpose: implement and test one coherent land-origin architecture focused on
authorable final land amount.

Work:

- predeclare the architecture, debug/development seed split, complete target
  sweep, failure gates, and evidence bundle;
- implement boundary-neutral parent formation, fixed-window identity,
  minimal physical land/water realization, and a deterministic causal target
  solve;
- expose all material and solver fields required for causal audit;
- produce diagnostic galleries through a predeclared development harness;
  the shared-UI adapter remains `ready: false` while its advertised
  fragmentation control is not implemented;
- evaluate targets 0 through 70 on the authoritative final mask;
- test monotonicity, same-seed structural continuity, determinism,
  resolution behavior, exact causal border, and parent-domain independence;
- conduct preliminary blind land/coast morphology review only after hard
  conformance produces judgeable maps.

Exit criteria:

- every predeclared development map satisfies its individual target band and
  exact water ring;
- target sweeps do not reverse beyond one final-mask cell of measurement
  tolerance and do not change latent world or window;
- no final-mask edits, seed retries, crop relocation, or numerical-boundary
  dependence;
- morphology is sufficiently plausible to justify adding the second control;
- the implementation and every failure are recorded without spending fresh
  validation seeds.

M1 outputs are scoped target-development evidence, not full-contract
deliverables. They do not establish fragmentation conformance or UI
readiness.

A failed M1 architecture is rejected or revised through a new registered
attempt; its acceptance criteria are not relaxed after inspection.

## M2 — fragmentation and target orthogonality

Purpose: make land organization authorable without converting fragmentation
into component surgery or a second land-amount control.

Work:

- predeclare the upstream fragmentation mechanism and ablation;
- run the common target × fragmentation matrix on the same seeds, latent
  fields, geometry, and windows;
- verify every matrix cell still satisfies its requested land band;
- measure macro-landmass dominance, component-area spectrum, dispersion,
  connectivity, and residual land drift;
- evaluate `F=0` as a cohort tendency toward a dominant macro-landmass where
  sufficient land exists, preserving valid small secondary islands;
- evaluate whether increasing F produces causally credible separation rather
  than lace, cut channels, deleted bridges, scattered stamps, or rerolls;
- conduct blind same-seed morphology and control-response review.

Exit criteria:

- land target remains conforming across the fragmentation range;
- fragmentation acts upstream, uses common latent randomness, and has a
  stable cohort-level response without a body-count promise;
- fixed-window, border, determinism, domain, resolution, and naturalness
  requirements remain satisfied;
- ablation and cost/yield evidence justify retaining the mechanism;
- every advertised control is implemented and causally wired before the
  adapter may be considered for `ready: true`.

## M3 — frozen validation and port-readiness

Purpose: determine whether the complete land-origin module is trustworthy on
fresh evidence and separable enough to port.

Before execution, freeze:

- implementation, defaults, controls, version, and supported geometries;
- untouched validation seeds;
- target/fragmentation/resolution matrix;
- all gates, instrument calibration, blind prompts, judges, and stop rules.

Run:

- complete per-map target and border checks;
- same-seed target/fragmentation authorability tests;
- deterministic replay, resolution comparison, and applicable
  parent/numerical-domain independence proof;
- blind perceptual morphology review with persisted evidence;
- performance and interface audit;
- final quarantine and dependency audit.

No validation result may cause tuning within the same run. Failure creates a
new development attempt and preserves the validation evidence.

Exit criteria:

- all hard contract requirements pass on the frozen cohort;
- calibrated control-response requirements pass;
- the author accepts the morphology;
- limitations are explicit;
- a port-readiness report defines inputs, outputs, causal fields,
  deterministic semantics, and integration risks without performing the
  port.
