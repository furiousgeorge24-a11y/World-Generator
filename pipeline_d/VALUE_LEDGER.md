# Value ledger — land-origin laboratory

This ledger starts clean. No mechanism, cost/yield conclusion, or historical
feature row is imported from `pipeline_b`.

Rows are added only when Pipeline C actually implements or materially tests a
mechanism. Predicted yield is written before reviewing its output; observed
yield and disposition are added afterward. The ledger recommends retention
or removal, but only the author decides.

An indispensable core mechanism uses a documented counterfactual or
calibrated probe rather than a fictitious user-facing off switch. Optional
mechanisms receive same-seed ablations wherever that comparison is meaningful.

## Required columns

| Mechanism | Attempt / milestone | Predicted contract yield | Runtime and complexity cost | Causal justification | Counterfactual / ablation | Observed evidence | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

No model mechanisms exist in M0, so there are currently no rows.

Likely future subjects include the parent-domain construction,
land-material/target controller, water-exposure solve, fragmentation process,
fixed-window scheme, and any representation or numerical method with material
effect on morphology. Their appearance here is not pre-approval; each needs a
registered attempt and evidence.

## Ledger rules

- State yield in terms of specific [`CONTRACT.md`](CONTRACT.md) promises.
- Separate numeric composition yield from perceptual morphology yield.
- Include worst relevant runtime tier and material implementation complexity.
- Record failed and negative-yield mechanisms; do not delete their rows.
- Link immutable reports, ablations, images, and author rulings.
- Mark supersession without rewriting the historical observed result.
- Keep experimental chronology in
  [`ATTEMPT_REGISTER.md`](ATTEMPT_REGISTER.md), not inside one feature row.
