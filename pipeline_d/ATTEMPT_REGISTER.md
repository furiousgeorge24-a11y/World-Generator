# Attempt and failure register — land-origin laboratory

This register starts clean. No model attempt, failure chronology, seed result,
or promotion conclusion is imported from `pipeline_b`.

M0 documentation and harness construction are bootstrap work, not a land-model
attempt. The first executed model hypothesis will receive ID `C00`.

## Acceptance dimensions

No attempt is promotable unless its declared scope closes every applicable
dimension together:

1. per-map final land amount within ±10 percentage points for the requested
   0–70 range;
2. same-seed target response that does not reverse beyond one final-mask cell
   of measurement tolerance, with common latent randomness and one fixed
   window;
3. exact final outer-ring water obtained without frame-aware formation or a
   corrective edge operation;
4. an applicable parent and numerical-domain independence proof;
5. plausibly natural macro-landmass morphology;
6. fragmentation behavior, once in scope, that operates upstream, tends as
   promised across the cohort, and remains approximately land-budget-
   orthogonal;
7. determinism, resolution behavior, complete evidence, and author review.

A focused diagnostic attempt may deliberately test only a subset, but it must
say so before execution and cannot claim overall acceptance.

## Registration rules

Before execution, each attempt records:

- unique sequential ID (`C00`, `C01`, ...), date, owner, and implementation
  hash;
- one falsifiable hypothesis and the exact mechanism allowed to change;
- debug, development, and untouched validation seed roles;
- physical geometry, resolutions, and exact target/fragmentation matrix;
- frozen hard gates, calibrated behavioral gates, diagnostics, and stop rules;
- expected artifacts and the decision that each possible outcome supports.

After execution, the same row records:

- what actually ran, including deviations and incomplete stages;
- all decisive successes and failures, not only the headline metric;
- whether the attempt was rejected, retained for development, promoted,
  superseded, stopped, or abandoned before execution;
- which narrow premises survive and which do not;
- immutable evidence paths and hashes;
- any model, default, public control, protocol, prompt, or adapter-readiness
  change.

## Seed and selection discipline

- A failed seed is not replaced.
- No hidden retries, best-of-many seed selection, or result-dependent seed
  expansion is permitted.
- Target and fragmentation variants reuse the same latent randomness and
  delivered window for each seed and geometry.
- Target-driven or fragmentation-driven crop search is prohibited.
- Debug seeds may inform implementation; development seeds may decide an
  architecture; validation seeds remain untouched until M3.
- An unexecuted implementation is recorded as abandoned or superseded before
  execution, never described as an empirical failure.

## Evidence qualifications

- Deterministic hard failures and perceptual morphology verdicts are recorded
  separately.
- Regular, straight, or frame-parallel geometry is a causal-audit trigger,
  not automatic evidence of a frame-aware process.
- A structural or coarse land proxy is never described as final land success.
- A map that passes composition but lacks causal/domain proof remains
  unresolved or failed according to its precommit; visual plausibility cannot
  rescue it.
- Sealed evidence is not rewritten after execution. Later reinterpretation is
  appended with its date, cause, and supersession effect.
