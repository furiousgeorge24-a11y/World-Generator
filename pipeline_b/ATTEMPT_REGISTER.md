# Private attempt and failure register — pipeline_b

This is the canonical cross-attempt index for the border, land-composition,
and coupled process-domain work through 2026-08-31.
[`MILESTONES.md`](MILESTONES.md) preserves the detailed historical narratives,
and [`VALUE_LEDGER.md`](VALUE_LEDGER.md) records feature cost/yield. Normative
acceptance rules live in [`CONTRACT.md`](CONTRACT.md) and
[`EVAL.md`](EVAL.md). This file exists so an unsuccessful or superseded path
cannot disappear between those longer records.

An accepted solution must eventually satisfy all four conditions together:

1. the exact outermost ring of the final delivered-resolution map is water;
2. formation and downstream processes are independent of the delivered frame
   and of any finite numerical rim that can affect the delivered result;
3. crop-last selection can reliably supply the accepted low 15–25%, medium
   30–40%, and high 45–<50% land bands; and
4. the available maps have plausibly natural morphology. Naturally formed
   frame-parallel features are valid; square, straight, or border-contouring
   features caused by construction or a frame-aware correction are not.

No attempt below has passed all four conditions.

## Audit qualifications

### Interpretation rules

- Historical contour-alignment gates are preserved as facts about what the
  old protocols did, but are not current acceptance criteria. Under the
  clarified causal rule, a naturally generated coastline or isobath may
  parallel the frame. The old gate cannot be used to reject B03 or the 15
  B04 alternatives; their unresolved numerical-domain and validation gaps
  remain.
- Continental-tag collars, clearance envelopes, and exterior-connectivity
  tests are conservative diagnostics, not substitutes for the exact final
  delivered outer ring. Pre-elevation B06–B08 records therefore contain no
  final water verdict.
- Sealed reports are not rewritten after execution. B12, B13, B14, B16, and
  B17 retain an `unreviewed` manual-status field even though a later
  `manual_review.md` exists beside each report. B12's review concerns only
  the parent layout and non-authoritative watch items because no qualifying
  final crop existed. For B09 and B11, the
  post-seal visual verdict is recorded in `MILESTONES.md` and here; no
  separate manual-review artifact was created at execution time.
- B00 and B02 were exploratory runs. Their images and sources survive, but
  their printed sweep tables were not serialized into sealed reports. Their
  numerical headlines must not be treated as independently replayable
  promotion evidence.
- The centered or movable rectangles in diagnostic images are annotations of
  the tested frame. They are not terrain operations. Only selectors explicitly
  identified above choose an origin, and all accepted crop-last protocols run
  selection after their authority geography freezes.
- The uniform nearshore bathymetric ramp and uniform-looking mountain flanks
  were investigated and deliberately deferred, not subjected to corrective
  attempts. Their findings remain in the corresponding active/watch rows of
  `VALUE_LEDGER.md`; they are not hidden failed solutions in this register.
- Instrument calibration (2026-08-31, `tests/geometry_instrument_checks.py`):
  the analytic-null D4 long-ruler gate fires on perfectly isotropic raster
  input (~0.70 near-D4 fraction, p ≈ 0), so the B16 and B17 D4 legs are not,
  by themselves, evidence of formation-caused grid alignment; both
  rejections continue to rest on their independent manual-morphology
  reviews, which are unaffected. Future D4 claims must use a matched
  isotropic baseline (see EVAL.md operational rules).

## Current architectural conclusion

The accumulated evidence rules out more parameter tuning of a finite-rim
central frame, point-nucleus arrival growth, convergence-as-final-mask, or a
localized marine transport law whose result depends on its rectangular solve
window. The premises still supported are: a boundaryless parent domain,
crop-last selection using exact final water, fixed physical land support,
convergence as a crust source, and natural material accretion that can create
broad two-dimensional rafts before any downstream terrain solve.

B18 (2026-09-01) adds: the approved M1 formation itself runs cleanly on the
boundaryless parent — rotation included — closing the program's structural
blind spot. Its geometry measures below the matched isotropic D4 baseline,
unlike every prior periodic formation. What it does NOT yet supply at
approved continental density is the medium/high land bands; that shortfall
is quantified and sits with the queued budget/nuclei author decision rather
than with the parent architecture.

## Untested alternative plans (reserved, not attempted)

Three plans were formulated on 2026-08-31 as the highest-likelihood routes
to all four acceptance conditions. Plan A became B18. The alternatives were
NOT tested and are preserved here so a future stall of Plan A does not have
to rediscover them:

- **Plan B — finite parent with quantified rim tolerance (fallback).** Keep
  the approved finite planar world, but MEASURE how far the numerical rim's
  influence propagates into the interior (paired builds at two world sizes;
  the disagreement front is the rim tolerance — quantified, not assumed),
  then place a sealed derived-window selector that stays inside the measured
  tolerance. Weaker than Plan A because rim independence is statistical
  rather than by construction, and the selector needs its own §3b
  ratification; cheaper because the approved engine runs unmodified. Falls
  back cleanly if the periodic port develops downstream problems (e.g. M2/M3
  stages that cannot consume a torus without their own finite windows).
- **Plan C — instrument calibration + sub-cell representation (merged into
  A's preliminaries; partially complete).** Two halves. (1) Calibrate the
  geometry gates before trusting any further run — DONE 2026-08-31
  (`eval/geometry_instruments.py` + 13/13 calibration suite; analytic-null
  D4 decommissioned as standalone evidence). (2) Sub-cell/continuous
  material representation so binary-lattice quantization stops manufacturing
  the artifacts the gates then condemn — independently SHIPPED by the M3
  sub-cell material snapshot for production; a periodic-parent equivalent
  rides free in B18 because the port keeps the approved supersampled
  snapshot. What remains untested from C: carrying continuous `cont_frac`
  (rather than the >=0.5 binary mask) through window selection and
  composition census.
